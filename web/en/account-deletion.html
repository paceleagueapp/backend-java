Account Deletion

App / Developer: paceleagueapp

To delete your Paceleague account, please follow the steps below.

How to Request Account Deletion

Step 1. Send an account deletion request to the following email address.
📧 paceleagueapp@gmail.com
Step 2. Include the following information in your email:
  • Username (ID)
  • Password
Step 3. We will process your request and notify you by email.

Data Retention Policy

  • Your data will be retained for 30 days after the deletion request to allow account recovery.
  • After 30 days, all personal data including username and nickname will be permanently deleted.
  • To recover your account within the 30-day period, contact us at the same email address.
