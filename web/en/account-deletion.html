Account Deletion

App / Developer: paceleagueapp

You can delete your Paceleague account directly inside the app.

How to Delete Your Account

Step 1. In the Paceleague app, go to Settings > Delete Account.
Step 2. Re-enter your password to confirm it is you.
Step 3. Confirm the deletion. It is processed immediately — no email request is needed.

Data Deletion Policy

  • When you delete your account, your personal data (nickname, email, password) and running / health data are deleted immediately.
  • Ranking history, land-grab (Landit) territories, and any uploaded images or videos are deleted as well.
  • Your username is replaced so it can be reused — you may sign up again with the same username.
  • Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone. There is no retention period or recovery process.
  • To preserve community context, posts and comments you already wrote may remain, shown anonymously as "Withdrawn user".
